About Quiz The Spire

Created and Owned by Lukas Bohez

Free quiz platforms, creator tools, and open-source apps — used in 95+ countries

Our Mission

Quiz The Spire is the home of Lukas Bohez's free tools and open-source apps — an independent, self-hosted product line built by one developer in Belgium. It covers a real-time multiplayer quiz platform, daily word puzzles, creator tools for MIDI and storytelling, and cross-platform media apps, all shipped to users in 95+ countries.

The thread running through everything is the same: interactive, social, and genuinely useful software that stays free and respects the people using it. No tracking, no locked-in features — just tools that work well and keep improving.

The Story Behind Quiz The Spire

Quiz The Spire started as a personal project — a real-time multiplayer quiz platform where teachers host sessions and students join with a room code. Its creator, Lukas Bohez, built it by hand in his own stack: a FastAPI/Python backend, a vanilla-JS frontend, and a self-hosted server he operates and maintains himself.

What started as a single quiz platform grew into a whole hub: a daily word puzzle (SENTLE), creator tools like MIDIComposer and Avatar Studio, an idle game, study guides, and open-source desktop apps like Convert The Spire Reborn / BitPlayer — a media downloader and torrent client shipped to Windows, Linux, macOS, and Android. Every tool stays free and open source.

What Makes Quiz The Spire Unique

🎮 Real-Time Multiplayer Quiz

SpireAI, the flagship quiz platform, lets teachers host live, real-time sessions that students join with a room code — competing on AI-generated and community themes with live leaderboards.

⚡ Open-Source Apps

Convert The Spire Reborn / BitPlayer is a cross-platform media downloader with a built-in torrent client and media player, shipped to Windows, Linux, macOS, and Android — free and GPLv3 licensed.

🌡️ Creator Tools

MIDIComposer turns a scene description into a full MIDI arrangement, Avatar Studio builds voice-over timelines, and Story Creator makes branching interactive stories — all free in the browser.

📚 Daily Puzzles & Study Aids

SENTLE is a daily Wordle-style sentence puzzle with global score sync, and the Rijexamen guide prepares Belgian learner drivers route by route for the practical exam.

🎯 Privacy-First by Default

No tracking on the tools, no accounts required for core features, and desktop apps that process everything locally — your files never leave your device.

🎨 A Growing Ecosystem

A command-palette homepage ties it all together, with an idle game, stock-prediction lab, article library, and new tools landing regularly — all maintained as one product.

Meet the Creator

Lukas Bohez - Founder & Developer

Lukas Bohez is the sole creator, owner, and operator of Quiz The Spire. He's a full-stack developer and indie creator from Belgium who designs, builds, hosts, and maintains the entire platform himself — from the Python/FastAPI and Go backends to the Flutter/Dart desktop apps.

Lukas's expertise spans:

  • Backend Systems: Python/FastAPI and Go services, WebSockets, SQL databases, and self-hosted Linux infrastructure
  • Frontend & Mobile: Next.js/TypeScript (Next.js 16), vanilla-JS tooling, and Flutter/Dart cross-platform apps for Windows, Linux, macOS, and Android
  • Media Engineering: yt-dlp pipelines, FFmpeg, MIDI/audio tooling, and local-first conversion work
  • UX & Accessibility: responsive, accessible interfaces that pass Lighthouse 100/100 on accessibility and perform well on mobile
  • Open Source: every project ships free and open source under GPLv3 or MIT, with CI, linting, and security hygiene maintained like a product

Beyond Quiz The Spire, Lukas shares a Figma plugin for Lottie import and AI-ready extraction, a driving-exam study guide, and a growing collection of games, tools, and articles — all under the same banner.
