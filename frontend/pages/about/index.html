About

This is a small personal site run by Lukas Bohez, a student developer in Belgium. It hosts a handful of tools I wrote: a media converter app, a browser MIDI tool, and a squad finder for Warframe.

Why the site looks different lately

Some older parts of this site (a quiz game, community articles and stories, an idle game, and a few other tools) were taken offline in September 2026 at the request of rights holders, and because parts of the written content on the site had been produced with AI tools and I can't stand behind the rights to it. That content is gone and won't be coming back.

What remains is the work I wrote and can vouch for.

How the site is built

The site is plain HTML and a bit of CSS, served by Apache from a Raspberry Pi at home. There is no framework, no build step for these pages, and no advertising.
