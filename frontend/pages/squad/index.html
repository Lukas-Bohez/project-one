About Squad Finder

Squad Finder helps you team up with other Tenno for Warframe missions. Browse open squads, create your own, or let Quick Match drop you straight into one.

Getting started

  • Enter your exact in-game name and press Connect. We remember you - next visit you're logged in automatically. Use Switch in the status bar to change Tenno.
  • Browse Available Squads on the left and filter by mission, planet, difficulty, region, mode and squad size.
  • Click a squad card to open it, say hi in Squad Chat, and coordinate before you group up in game.

Creating your own squad

  • Pick a name, mission type, planet, difficulty, region and language, choose a size and mode, then hit Create Squad.
  • Mission type lists real gamemodes and special activities only: Survival, Disruption, EDA (Deep Archimedea), ETA (Temporal Archimedea), Cascade (Level Cap), Archon Hunt, Duviri, Perrita Rebellion, Descendia and more.
  • Modifiers are separate checkboxes: tick Steel Path, Nightmare and/or Void Fissure on top of the mission instead of hunting for them in the mission list.
  • Objective tells people what the run is for: Clear (finish it), Farm (grind loot/points) or Other.
  • Description is a freeform line for everything else: "EDA Stella farm, Cyte-09 ad clear, need supports" - say exactly what you want.
  • Squad size: 4 Players (Quick) for classic squads, 6 Players (Standard) for full six-Tenno teams. 6-player squads always run one fixed mission / planet / difficulty, so those filters don't apply to them.
  • Mode: Casual for relaxed runs and learning, Serious for focused, efficient play.
  • As leader (crown badge) you can Update Settings anytime or Kick Player if someone disrupts the squad.

Roles

  • Once you're in a squad, pick your Your role: DPS, Support, Buffer, Shield, Arcane, Resource, Efficiency or Any.
  • Your role shows as a badge next to your name, so leaders can see at a glance if the squad covers what the run needs - one unique Warframe per slot runs start here.

Quick Match

  • Choose what you feel like playing - or leave everything on Any - and press Find Match.
  • You're dropped into a random open squad that matches your settings. If nothing fits, one is created for you automatically.
  • Region is the strongest filter - squads in your region are matched first, so set it to your ping zone.

Ready up & etiquette

  • Press Mark Ready when you're set - the green dots next to names show who's good to go.
  • Use chat for loadouts, builds and "inv me in game."
  • Leave Squad is always free, no penalty. Nothing here launches your game for you - you still group up in Warframe itself.

Trust score - how it works

Every player has a trust score - it's the shield number next to their name. Everyone starts at 50, right in the middle.

The shield colour tells you at a glance: 70+ green, 40-69 orange, below 40 red.

Scores stay between 0 to 100. Every clean mission moves you up by +0.5, and reports from other players are saved for moderator review instead of moving the score automatically. Run missions with your squad, help your teammates, and your score climbs. Be disruptive, and moderators can step in. Simple as that.

Stay safe

  • Never share passwords, real names or payment details - not even in squad chat, and beware of anyone offering "free Platinum".
  • For serious runs, prefer squads led by high-trust players.
  • Griefing gets people kicked and reported. Reports go to moderator review, so bad actors don't last.
Pro tip: set your region to your ping zone and leave filters on "Any" - Quick Match will find you the fastest squad.
