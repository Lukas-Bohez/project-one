How to Play Sentle

Guess the daily sentence, one word at a time — it assembles itself in the banner as you solve it.

  • You'll see one word at a time, its length shown as empty tiles
  • You have 4 attempts per word
  • After each guess, letters change color:
    • 🟩 Green: Letter in correct position
    • 🟨 Yellow: Letter in word but wrong position
    • ⬜ Gray: Letter not in word
  • Solve a word and it locks permanently into the sentence banner, then the next word unlocks
  • Out of attempts? The word auto-reveals (dashed outline) and locks anyway — the game always moves forward
  • Auto-revealed words don't count toward a perfect-game score

A new sentence is available each day at midnight!
